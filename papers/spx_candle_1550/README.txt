The 15:50 Candle
================

A shorter journal version (16 pages, 10 November 2026) is in
../spx_candle_1550_journal/; this working paper is its Internet
Appendix. See that folder's README.txt.

Thirteenth update, 10 November 2026: a new Section 15,
"Closing-auction imbalances", from a pre-registered study (plan
preregistration/spx_candle_imbalance.md, locked 2026-11-10 19:37 UTC,
one registered run). On the rule's 644 signal days of 2021-2026 it
bought the closing-auction imbalance messages that the NYSE and Nasdaq
sent from 15:50 for SPY's 50 largest holdings (Databento, XNYS.PILLAR
and XNAS.ITCH) and asked whether the candle is right more often on
days when it points the same way as the summed 15:50 imbalance. It is
not: the candle agreed with the imbalance on 72.8% of days, but was
right on 55.9% of agreeing days against 61.7% of disagreeing days
(d -5.9 points, t -1.35; block-bootstrap interval -14.8 to +4.0), so
H1 did not pass, and the imbalance direction alone was right on 51.1%.
The section uses the plan's wording for a failed test (not confirmed;
limited power; does not refute the auction explanation). Two tables
(the tests with the robustness checks, and agreement by year) and the
prose were generated by backtests/spx_candle_imbalance/make_tex.py
(imbalance_section_draft.tex). The abstract, introduction, data table,
table of studies, discussion, negative results and conclusion now say
so; no earlier result changed. 36 pages.

Reproducing the results
-----------------------
Run from the repository root with the project's virtual
environment. Each pre-registered study checks that its plan's hash
is in preregistration/LEDGER.md and refuses to read real data
otherwise. The limit-order script has no plan.

.venv/bin/python -B backtests/spx_candle_scan/scan.py discover
.venv/bin/python -B backtests/spx_candle_scan/scan.py holdout

.venv/bin/python -B analysis/candle_1550_forward.py
.venv/bin/python -B analysis/candle_1550_options_forward.py

.venv/bin/python -B backtests/spx_candle_exits/exits.py run
